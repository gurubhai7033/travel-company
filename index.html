About ExploreEra

Founded in 2010, ExploreEra has grown from a small local travel agency to an internationally recognized travel company with offices in 12 countries.

Our mission is to create meaningful travel experiences that connect people with cultures, nature, and each other. We believe that travel has the power to change lives and broaden perspectives.

With a team of over 200 travel experts who have visited more than 150 countries, we bring firsthand knowledge and passion to every itinerary we create.
